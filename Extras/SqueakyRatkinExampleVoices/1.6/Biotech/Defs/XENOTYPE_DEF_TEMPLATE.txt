This directory is documentation only. LoadFolders.xml intentionally does not load 1.6/Biotech,
and this Template package ships no Xenotype XML, PackDef, SoundDef, targetDefName, or audio here.

To create a separate Xenotype voice pack, give that package its own packageId, globally unique
SR_ PackDef/SoundDef names, and its own package-specific audio root. Add a Biotech-gated load
folder in that separate package, then define exactly one <scope>Xenotype</scope> PackDef with
one exact, case-sensitive <targetDefName>YOUR_XENOTYPE_DEFNAME</targetDefName>. Do not reuse
SR_ExampleTemplate_Race, this package's audio paths, or its identity.

The action folders beside this file are empty naming skeletons only. Replace their TXT markers
with audio you have the right to distribute in your own independently identified package.
